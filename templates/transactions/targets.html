How these are used

A target entered here is compared with the commission secured in that period — the net commission on transactions that completed in it.

A quarter or a year you leave empty is added up from the months inside it. Enter one of your own to override that.

Clearing a box removes that target. The chart then shows “no target set” for the period rather than nought.
